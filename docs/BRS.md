# Business Requirements Specification – PipeRisk

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-1 | Rank areas of the network by the probability of at least one main break in the coming year. |
| BR-2 | Report whether the predicted probabilities are calibrated over later years, and the effect of recalibration. |
| BR-3 | Compare against simple break-count baselines before claiming any benefit from a model. |
| BR-4 | State that the data hold only break locations and dates (no pipe age, material, diameter) and make no claim about causes. |

Out of scope: pipe-level risk, consequence of failure, replacement planning, weather or soil covariates.
